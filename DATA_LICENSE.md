The application data originate from CLEAR Global and its partners' safeguarding and PSEA glossary. Source: https://clearglobal.org/safeguarding-and-psea/ . Preserve that attribution and cite the accompanying research paper when using this research implementation.

No explicit data license or redistribution-permission record was included in the supplied archives. The data have been bundled here for the release owner's review. Before publishing or redistributing them, confirm and document the applicable terms or permission from the data owner. Do not label the glossary as Apache-2.0 solely because the code uses that license.
